Assigned Case

Case G: Kehoe's Garage, Enniscorthy, Co. Wexford.

Hunch (before AI)

I believe small independent garages would pay for this app/website in order to optimise their business and improve customer satisfaction.

Chosen Niche

Small independent garages in Ireland with approximately 2–6 mechanics and a small office or reception team.

Buyer: The garage owner or manager.

Daily users: Mechanics and office/reception staff. Customers also use the customer-facing status screen to check the progress of their own vehicle.

Pain

Pain (invented): “We waste too much time answering calls from customers asking if their car is ready, and when the workshop gets busy, jobs waiting for parts can easily be overlooked.”
Pain (invented): “We need a simple way for the workshop and office to see where every car is without constantly stopping work to ask each other.”

Technology and Current Process

Technology: A web-based workshop job tracking and customer status system.

Current process (assumption): Small independent garages may manage jobs using paper job cards, diaries, whiteboards, spreadsheets and phone calls. Staff may need to manually check the progress of a vehicle when customers contact the garage for an update.

Existing Products

1. Motorworks

Motorworks is garage management software for independent Irish workshops. Its website describes features including digital job cards, workshop management and a customer portal.

Business Model

Assumption: €199 one-time setup fee and €49 per month.

Reasoning: The setup fee covers the initial setup of the system for the garage, while the monthly subscription provides a predictable recurring cost that is more accessible to a small independent garage than a large upfront software investment.

Lean PRD

Hypothesis: Small independent garages will be interested in a simple system that reduces customer status calls and makes it easier to track the progress of vehicles through the workshop.

Audience: Small independent garages in Ireland with approximately 2–6 mechanics and a small office or reception team.

Call to action: Join the waiting list.

Measure of success: The percentage of landing-page visitors who choose to join the waiting list.

Out of scope: Online payments, invoicing, parts ordering, staff scheduling and full garage-management/accounting functions are not included in the alpha.

Alpha Test

I tested the alpha on my phone by moving vehicles through the workshop stages, refreshing the page to confirm that changes were saved, checking the office view and searching for a vehicle through the customer view.

The main issue I found was that I entered “Job K1002” instead of “K1002”, which returned no result even though the vehicle existed. This showed that the customer search could be confusing if a customer included the word “Job”.

I changed the search so that it accepts the job number with or without the word “Job”. I tested the customer search again after the change to confirm that it worked.

AI Use

Generative AI was used after the initial hunch to help develop the niche, product thinking, HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the alpha and landing page, and to troubleshoot the customer search during testing. The published pages and functionality were reviewed and tested before submission.
