GarageFlow is a simple workshop job tracker that helps small independent garages keep staff and customers updated on where each vehicle is in the repair process.

Join the waiting list Try the demo
Less chasing. More fixing.

Know where every car is without stopping the workshop to ask.

When job updates live on paper, whiteboards or in people's heads, office staff may have to interrupt mechanics just to answer a customer's status question. GarageFlow gives the workshop, office and customer a simple view of the same repair journey.

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Update the job

Workshop staff tap a large button to move a vehicle through each repair stage without typing lengthy updates.

2

Keep the office informed

Office staff can see vehicles grouped by stage, including jobs that have been waiting too long for parts.

3

Let customers check

Customers use their job number and registration to check the current stage of their own vehicle without seeing anyone else's information.
